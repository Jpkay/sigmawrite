# Son/sont and on/ont recognition pools

Adds 32 original recognition questions, sixteen per pair, with eight correct uses of each spelling in each pool. Contexts include son before feminine nouns beginning with a vowel, sont as a copula and auxiliary, on with singular agreement, and ont expressing possession and forming compound tenses. These remain questions about applying the distinction in sentences, not independent writing.

Every item records both offered word forms and its displayed sentence. A completed sentence is not invented as delivered material: the student sees a gap and the choices. Correct positions are balanced. All items remain needs_human_review.

The approved evidence rule requires novel target words. A fixed pair cannot supply the required number of distinct target words, even with new sentences. The pre-existing homophone feasibility audit describes the conflict. This batch does not change that rule, remove word exposure, or silently activate the targets. Prepared scope stays 344/542; these pools remain outside the release scope.
