# Passé récent verb-family production

Four lessons and 48 original short sentence applications assess the regular -er and -ir families and the -ger and -cer families independently. Each family has twelve application contexts covering all six persons. The approved prerequisites, present-tense venir and recognition of the passé récent, remain required and available.

Teaching explains a recently completed action through venir in the present, de or d’, and an unchanged infinitive. It distinguishes this construction from venir used for movement. Guided exercises use separate sentences and keep each exercise's own verb. Their answers are instruction, never independent mastery evidence.

The tense and infinitive are supplied. These questions assess controlled form production, not spontaneous tense selection or new vocabulary. Five distinct written responses are possible across the six persons because je and tu both use viens. The response-space annotation therefore uses five unique alternatives and a conservative one-in-five guessing floor; this is not an empirical calibration estimate.
