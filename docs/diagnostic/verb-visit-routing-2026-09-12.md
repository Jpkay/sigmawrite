# Verb coverage within the diagnostic time budget

A verb's bounded visit now counts questions across all its tense categories. Previously each category had its own counter, so changing tense could postpone rotation to another verb repeatedly.

Conjugation scheduling first gives each available family (general concepts, regular patterns, individual verbs) at least three questions. After that initial survey, individual verbs receive twice the scheduling weight of either other family. Their separate identities need their own evidence. This weighting changes neither the total time budget nor domain/strand balance, evidence requirements, chance estimates or mastery decisions.

The mixed verb-frontier simulation now reaches avoir in the passé composé, where it records two incorrect responses, after previously sampling only aller among individual verbs. Two errors are not enough to establish the requested within-verb boundary: the full symbolic benchmark remains at 10/10 depth checks and 6/8 discrimination checks. The verb-specific tense-frontier and reading-reference-gap contrasts remain unresolved, and no violation or fabricated mastery is reported. This is a bounded coverage improvement, not proof of complete diagnostic validity.

Validation: 556 granular tests across 161 files pass, including the initial family sampling guarantees, shared visit counting across forms, and sampling at least two individual verbs within the prepared full-graph time budget. The symbolic benchmark retains the approved 542-target graph and the 35-minute budget. All unanswered skills remain unknown.

This runtime change is prepared locally. It does not change the frozen 197-target candidate currently undergoing its full browser journey.
