# Dire and prendre: revision 35 draft

Adds separate controlled-production lessons for dire and prendre, each with six guided exercises and twelve independent sentence checks across all six persons. Original short narrative contexts distinguish lesson material from assessment sentences. The tense is supplied; choosing a tense in independent writing remains a separate target.

The forms were checked against the Académie française conjugation tables for [dire](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9D2606) and [prendre](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9P4070). The dire lesson explains that singular dis/dis/dit also occur in the present, so form alone cannot identify the tense; it contrasts present vous dites with passé simple vous dîtes. The prendre lesson distinguishes il prit from il a pris and preserves prîmes/prîtes accents. These are original explanations and examples, not copied dictionary exercises.

The new revision 35 draft has 336 of 542 targets with assessment and teaching coverage, and 6,290 in-scope questions. Twelve exact-target lesson gaps remain, including eight passé simple verbs. Revisions 33 and 34 remain immutable; revision 35 has not been published or deployed. Human review remains pending.

All 374 test files / 1,671 tests and TypeScript passed after regenerating the matching consolidated revision 35 bank.
