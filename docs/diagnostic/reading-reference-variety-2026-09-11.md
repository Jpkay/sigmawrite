# Additional narrative pronoun-reference checks

Eight new fictional passages, each fewer than 65 words, extend the existing narrative object-pronoun reference pool. The target already had a lesson and questions; this is additional material for later checks, not an additional supported skill. New passages assess le, la, les, lui and leur, including a feminine recipient for lui. The existing lesson is reused without duplicating its target or content identifier.

The new passages remain needs_human_review. Answer and textual-support choices are authored explicitly; test fixtures pin the intended referents, source anchoring and separation from guided lesson passages. These checks do not establish that a student can produce pronouns in writing or comprehend other text genres.

Validation: 469 granular tests passed, TypeScript and targeted ESLint passed. Prepared supported scope remains 154 targets with no instruction or fresh-check gaps.
