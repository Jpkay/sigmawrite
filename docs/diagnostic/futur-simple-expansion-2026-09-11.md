# Individual futur simple production

Adds fourteen exact-verb lessons for être, avoir, aller, faire, prendre, venir, partir, sortir, dire, voir, pouvoir, vouloir, savoir and devoir. Each teaches the future stem and six person endings, with six guided exercises. Regular infinitive stems, dropped final e and irregular stems are explained separately. Recognition, supplied-tense production and contextual tense choice remain distinct claims.

The question expansion adds 168 sentence gaps, twelve per verb, with independent answer paradigms checked in tests. All 3,032 previous conjugation expansion items remain byte-equivalent as parsed objects. Taught corrections and assessment sentence identities are checked for overlap; each new lesson has fresh follow-up questions. No human approval is invented.

Preparation now covers 96 assessment/teaching targets and 1,616 questions, with 102 draft lessons and 559 guided exercises in the catalogue. All 542 graph targets remain present. The full graph is still not covered. These draft additions are not yet in the published 66-target release.

Validation: 415 granular tests, TypeScript and targeted ESLint pass. Six symbolic profiles complete at 35 active minutes with 61 questions and no missing activities or binding violations. Server-command all-wrong and mixed journeys reach results; the all-wrong journey completes a guided lesson and an independent check. These simulations validate mechanics, not calibrated difficulty or assessment accuracy in real students.
