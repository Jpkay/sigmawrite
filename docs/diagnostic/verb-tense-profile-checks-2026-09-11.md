# Separate verb and tense evidence

Six regression profiles exercise the current question pools and activity bindings:

- Present être successful, present avoir unsuccessful, and the reverse.
- Aller successful in the imparfait but unsuccessful in the futur simple, and the reverse.
- Futur simple prendre successful and venir unsuccessful.
- Imparfait voir successful and faire unsuccessful.

The tests feed prescribed synthetic answers from the actual initial pools, with explicitly assumed earlier foundation evidence. They verify separate skill probabilities, a provisional gap for the weaker target, a lesson bound to that exact target, no remedial lesson for the stronger target, and no inferred evidence for untouched savoir in the futur simple. One assessment occasion does not become confirmed mastery.

All six tests pass, together with TypeScript and source lint. These are aggregation and lesson-routing regressions. They do not establish that the adaptive selector samples every declared contrast during an initial sitting, nor do they replace student calibration. The separate live browser test exercises question selection, grading and persistence.
