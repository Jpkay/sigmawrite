Added sixteen independent sentence contexts using other verbs: eight -geons and eight -çons forms with nous. Each answer is independently authored, checked against the deterministic conjugator, and tested against the corresponding missing-e or missing-cedilla error.

These explicit verb examples map only to the existing present-tense pattern facets. They do not add graph nodes, populate every other tense, or grant mastery of a different tense. Existing lessons retain their eight guided exercises each. Both now have eight available fresh check questions under the current allocation.
