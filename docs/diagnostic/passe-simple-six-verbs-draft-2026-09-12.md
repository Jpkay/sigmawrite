# Six remaining verb-specific passé simple lessons

Revision 35 now includes separate controlled-production lessons for partir, sortir, pouvoir, vouloir, savoir and devoir. Each adds six original guided exercises and twelve assessment sentences covering all six persons. This closes the six remaining exact-target passé simple lesson gaps in the current graph inventory; it does not imply coverage of every French verb or every use of past narration.

Forms were checked against the Académie française tables: [partir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9P0787), [sortir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9S2177), [pouvoir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9P3829), [vouloir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9V1230), [savoir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9S0657), [devoir](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9D2262). Explanations and narrative sentences are original. The ordinary movement/object-removal sense of sortir is used, not the distinct legal verb.

The lessons distinguish present forms, compound past forms and meaningful accents. Vouloir examples include intentions that fail, preventing an implication that wanting guarantees success. Savoir examples include learning information and successfully handling a situation. Devoir examples express necessity. All prompts supply the verb and tense, so these are form-production checks, not evidence of independent tense selection in writing.

Prepared scope is 344 of 542 targets, with 6,386 in-scope questions. Four exact-target lesson gaps remain: passé récent vouloir/savoir/devoir and imperative devoir. The other unfinished categories remain 187 question-pool gaps, two prerequisite-scope gaps and five connected-writing gaps. Published revision 34 is unchanged; revision 35 remains a draft and carries no invented human approval.
