# Conditionnel présent recognition and individual forms

Adds a recognition lesson with twelve contrast questions, plus fourteen individual-verb lessons, 84 guided production exercises and 168 independent sentence gaps. Recognition practice contrasts the futur simple, imparfait and selected compound conditional forms. It includes the use of the conditional as future viewed from the past, avoiding the false rule that it always expresses politeness or a hypothetical wish.

The exact-verb targets cover être, avoir, aller, faire, prendre, venir, partir, sortir, dire, voir, pouvoir, vouloir, savoir and devoir. Each has six-person paradigms verified against independently specified answer tables and fresh sentence material. The prerequisite recognition lesson is included before those targets can pass publication readiness. Existing conjugation and recognition item contents are preserved.
