# Identifying a supporting reason

Eight original short arguments add `identifier_raison_argument::argumentative-receptive::text_type:argumentative`. The approved prerequisite remains identifying the thesis. The student must identify the author's reason and select textual support, separately from the claim, contextual details and an opposing opinion. Some reasons have a connective and others do not. Each passage has fewer than 65 words.

The corresponding draft lesson contains six guided exercises on three other passages. No assessment text is reused from the thesis questions or either lesson. These questions assess recognition of the reason actually given, not factual truth, persuasiveness or independent argument writing.
