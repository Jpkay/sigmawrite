# Recognizing relative clauses

The approved graph's construction_subordonnee_relative reading-analysis target had two eligible questions and a generic database instruction card. The reuse audit marks that card for replacement; it gives neither a concrete relative clause nor usable practice.

The new draft lesson starts with a noun and the words that describe it, then names the relative clause, antecedent and relative pronoun. It covers qui, que, où and dont with an expressed noun. Six guided questions include nonrelative clauses. Sixteen independent assessment sentences provide three examples per pronoun and four actual absence cases: a complement of a verb, a time clause, an interrogative and a cause clause. Negative evidence is annotated rather than inferred from incorrect spelling. This recognition target does not claim sentence production or coverage of relatives without expressed antecedents or with lequel.

The initial and later question pools pass the approved evidence requirements and retain questions not shown in the lesson. Preparation now supports 97 targets with 103 draft lessons and 565 guided exercises. All 542 graph targets remain present. This is preparation for a later bank revision, not a modification to the frozen 96-target revision currently importing.
