# Verb-family recognition draft

The approved French node classer_famille_verbale currently lacks a complete question pool. This standalone expansion supplies 24 original MCQs: eight regular -er verbs, eight verbs following finir, and eight other verbs. Each question supplies the infinitive and first-person singular/plural present forms. It assesses recognition of a pattern, not the ability to produce conjugations unaided.

Choices name the chanter and finir models instead of numbering groups. The category exemplars are not themselves quiz answers. Aller includes je vais as well as nous allons; partir contrasts with grandir instead of treating every -ir verb alike. The third category has no exemplar that would directly reveal a target answer.

Classification reference: [OQLF, Verbes réguliers et irréguliers](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/24654/la-grammaire/le-verbe/conjugaison/verbes-reguliers-et-irreguliers). The questions and examples are original. The named-model approach avoids depending on differences in school group numbering.
