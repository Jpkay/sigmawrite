# Savoir imperative: unpublished draft

Adds an exact-target lesson for `produire_imperatif::verb:savoir`, with six guided exercises and twelve separate check sentences. Each of sache, sachons and sachez has two guided examples and four check sentences. The lesson distinguishes drawing attention to information from inviting a shared attitude, without implying that a person can acquire knowledge instantly on command.

The [Académie française conjugation reference](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/conjuguer/A9S0657) supports these three forms. The contexts are original drafts pending human review; reference consultation is not an approval.

Tests cover exact sentence separation, balanced persons, initial/follow-up pool allocation, requested-form validation, and rejection of sais/saches, savons/sachions and savez/sachiez for the corresponding imperative prompts. The meaning-pathway test now confirms the two remaining prerequisite gaps: vouloir and devoir. It does not remove those dependencies.
