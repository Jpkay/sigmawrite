# Subjunctive verb-family teaching drafts

Four unpublished lessons cover regular -er, the finir model, -ger and -cer. They contain 28 guided exercises and remain outside the registered catalogue. The frozen 253-target candidate is unchanged.

The lessons explain the -iss- extension for the finir model, the shared endings, and the difference between nous mangeons/lançons at the indicative present and que nous mangions/lancions at the subjunctive present. Guided sentences explicitly require the subjunctive. The boundary distinguishes supplied-mode form production from choosing a mode in connected writing and excludes aller and partir from the regular models.

Three focused tests verify exact graph targets, authored guided-answer paradigms and elision of que before subordinate pronouns. TypeScript checking passes. These tests do not imply human review or readiness for release.
