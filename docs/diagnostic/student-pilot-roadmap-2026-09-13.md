# French assessment and student pilot roadmap

The objective remains a granular, adaptive diagnostic built on the approved French graph, followed by an appropriate learning pathway. The initial sitting lasts 30–40 active minutes, supports pause/resume, and produces a provisional map. Learning starts immediately and continues to refine unresolved skills. The owner reviews content alongside student use. A technical end-to-end pass is not proof of educational accuracy.

## Ordered delivery milestones

### 1. Make provisional results understandable

Show the evidence supporting each assessed skill, alongside its provisional status. Preserve separate modes and specific graph targets. Distinguish correct answers from confirmed mastery and untested skills from difficulties. Writing-token accuracy must not become a fictitious count of correct essays.

Completion evidence: render mixed, successful, untested and writing cases; verify the displayed counts against the saved eligible evidence; record the exact displayed wording before delivery; verify the deployed result and progress pages. Deployed on 13 September: the technical-account check verified counts for 19 skills on results and progress without changing assessment state. See `skill-evidence-display-2026-09-13.md`. Teacher and student interpretation still needs pilot feedback.

### 2. Verify adaptive behavior across granular learner profiles

Use a matrix of contrasting patterns across verb families, individual verbs where represented by the approved graph, tense and agreement distinctions, grammar constructions, spelling, and short-passage reading. Include recognition versus production, misconceptions, guessing, skipped answers, inconsistent answers, and improvement after teaching. Exercise both upward and downward difficulty changes within the agreed time budget.

Completion evidence: reproducible traces show the questions asked, the exact skill each answer supports, remaining uncertainty, and why the first activity was chosen. Assessors must not infer mastery of an untested prerequisite or neighboring skill. Publish discrepancies as issues and fix them before treating those profiles as validated. Synthetic profiles are technical checks; teacher comparison with real students is still required.

### 3. Complete the prior-material evidence contract

Finish recording relevant reading, exercise, correction and reference material across the remaining student routes and client versions. Ensure unfamiliar-word or sentence requirements use verified history and exact source matches. Preserve old students' incomplete-history status rather than claiming retrospective coverage.

Completion evidence: route inventory gaps resolved, database atomicity and concurrent delivery checked, old-client and offline paths accounted for, and a fresh covered student independently verified. Enable complete-history claims only after this evidence exists. The prepared atomic migration and disabled contract are not completion evidence by themselves.

### 4. Complete graph and learning coverage

Add questions and teaching activities for the remaining 184 targets in batches tied to the approved French graph. Cover contrasting cases and difficulty ranges; keep guided material distinct from independent checks. Keep owner-review status explicit.

Completion evidence: a coverage report names every target, its eligible diagnostic questions, independent learning checks, available lessons and review status. No target becomes supported solely because a label or placeholder exists. Each released batch passes content validation, adaptive tests and deployed checks.

### 5. Run the supervised pilot and compare with teacher judgment

The pilot may begin before all 544 targets are supported, with its supported scope and provisional results clearly described. The owner reviews in parallel. Teachers compare the observed student performance with the skill map and first recommended activities; student feedback identifies unclear instructions, inappropriate difficulty and excessive duration.

Completion evidence: documented real-student journeys, teacher disagreements tracked and addressed, reliable saved work, suitable next activities, and clear handling of uncertainty. Do not label the assessment calibrated based only on automated tests.
