# Gate 5 psychometrics

No item, edge, or misconception statistic is interpreted below 30 observations.
At or above that threshold, items are flagged when p-value is below 0.20, above
0.95, or point-biserial discrimination is negative. Prerequisite edges are
flagged when mastered-vs-unmastered dependent-success lift is below 0.05.
Misconceptions require 30 selections before being confirmed or rejected.

Observed difficulty is blended into the deterministic prior with a maximum
10-point adjustment per run, so a text/item cannot jump more than one band.
Flags enter human review queues; the job never deletes content or edges.
