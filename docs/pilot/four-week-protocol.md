# Four-week pilot protocol

## Week 0 — readiness

Verify invitation/guardian authorization, adult logins, class codes, two competency slices, content
coverage, moderation, email delivery and rollback contacts. Record baseline
reading band and diagnostic frontier.

## Week 1 — activation

Target: 90% onboarding completion, 85% diagnostic completion, and 80% of pupils
completing a first session. Teachers review low-engagement pupils after day 3.

## Weeks 2–3 — learning loop

Target three sessions per active pupil per week, retrieval completion above 60%,
and median success inside 80–85%. Review content/moderation exceptions daily;
review graph blockers and intervention groups weekly.

## Week 4 — evidence and trust

Target parent-report open rate above 60%, teacher export/use above 70%, no
critical safety or privacy incident, and a measurable increase in mastered or
ready-to-learn nodes. Collect structured teacher/parent feedback without adding
student free text to analytics.

Stop conditions: critical privacy/security finding, unsafe published content,
systematic scoring defect, or authorization-gate bypass.
